Simplifying and Improving
On-Policy Distillation for Few-Step Diffusion

Abstract

Few-step distillation reduces the sampling cost of diffusion and flow models, but existing methods often rely on auxiliary score models or specialized architectures. We show that a simple on-policy regression baseline already produces high-quality and diverse samples in 4 NFE by matching teacher velocities along student rollouts.

A lightweight Pivot proxy models local velocity variations during training, while a repulsive regularizer uses weaker predictions from the same teacher as negative references. Simplified Matching Distillation remains entirely regression-based and leaves the generator architecture and sampling procedure unchanged. We evaluate image generation and editing, 3D generation, video synthesis, and joint audio–video generation, including cross-architecture and multi-teacher distillation.
